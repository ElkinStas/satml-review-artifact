At some steps the system will surface "failure-pattern cards" relevant to your
current observations, under a fenced block titled "Pattern cards (advisory)". Each
card has the shape {family, mechanism, applicability_signal, recommended_validation
_action}. Cards are advisory: you decide whether a card applies to the binary in
front of you. When you act on a card, pass its card_id in the cites_card_ids
argument of record_hypothesis.
