submit_candidate is gated, and the gate reads your DERIVATION, not your prose.

A submission is BLOCKED unless both hold:
  - the value you submit was RECONSTRUCTED from what the program computes -- by inverting its
    transform, solving its constraint, or emulating its checker. A value you simply found written
    somewhere and carried over does not qualify, however plausible it looks and even if it turns
    out to be right; AND
  - a record_validation names this exact candidate and cites (source_step_idx) the earlier analysis
    step that produced it.

The gate never learns whether your answer is correct, and neither do you: it checks only that a
derivation exists. Correct work that leads to a wrong answer passes; a right answer with no working
does not. Blocking is automatic and the error names exactly what is missing.
